Frequently Asked Questions

A few things people ask before their first IPL Photofacial.

What is a Skin Brightening Facial with IPL?+
It's a light-based treatment that uses Intense Pulsed Light to target excess pigment and redness beneath the skin's surface. As treated cells clear over the following days, your complexion looks noticeably brighter and more even.
Is IPL safe for fair and sensitive skin?+
Yes — our IPL protocols are specifically suited to fair and light-to-medium skin tones. During your consultation, we'll assess your skin type and sun exposure history to set the safest, most effective settings for you.
Is there any downtime after treatment?+
Most clients return to normal activities right away. Skin may look slightly pink or flushed for a few hours, and treated brown spots often darken briefly before flaking off naturally over the following one to two weeks.
How many sessions will I need?+
It depends on your goals. A single session can refresh overall tone, while sun damage, melasma, or broken capillaries typically respond best to a short series of sessions spaced several weeks apart.
When will I see results?+
Many clients notice brighter, more even skin within a week as treated pigment surfaces and clears. Fuller results for sun damage and hyperpigmentation usually build gradually over the course of your treatment plan.
